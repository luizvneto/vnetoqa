Services

I work as an independent Senior QA Engineer, available for contract and consulting engagements with product teams — most often based in the United States — while working from Brazil. Here's where I can help.

Test strategy & planning

Defining what to test, how, and why — so the whole team knows where quality risk actually lives before a single test case is written.

Manual & exploratory testing

Hands-on testing that catches what automation and specs miss, especially early in a feature's life.

Test automation support

Working alongside your engineers to build and maintain automated coverage that the team actually keeps up with.

QA process consolidation

Bringing fragmented testing practices across squads into one process people understand and actually follow.

Team mentorship & coaching

Helping QA engineers and cross-functional teammates grow their own testing instincts, not just follow a checklist.

Stakeholder communication

Acting as the bridge between engineering, product, and business — translating quality risk into decisions.

Engagement models

A few common ways companies bring me in — happy to shape something that fits your situation.

Project-based contract

Scoped, time-boxed engagements for a specific release, migration, or QA audit.

Fractional QA lead

Ongoing part-time involvement, guiding QA strategy without a full-time hire.

Long-term retainer

Continuous QA partnership embedded in your regular sprint cycle.
